Data & Methodology

Data Sources

Trade quantities and values are parsed directly from FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization) and UN Comtrade raw datasets. Specifically, we aggregate Item 656 (Green Bean Exports) and Item 657 (Roasted/Processed Coffee Exports).

Transformation Index (TI)

The Transformation Index determines a country's status as a raw producer vs. a processing hub. It is calculated by mathematically summing all raw coffee imports and subtracting raw coffee exports. A high net positive TI indicates a Processing Hub, while a negative TI designates a primary Producer.

Sankey Flow Definitions

Producers → Processors: Raw green bean exports.
Processors → Consumers: Roasted/processed coffee exports only.
Note: A country is categorized as a Processor visually in the Sankey chart if ≥30% of its total coffee exports are roasted.

Global Weighted Prices & 3D Math

The timeline's Global weighted average price (USD/tonne) divides total worldwide trade value by total worldwide volume for that specific year. The map's 3D extrusion (depth) uses a normalization scale matching maximum export volume, while mesh colors run through a power-curve interpolation applied to the Transformation Index to increase visual contrast.
